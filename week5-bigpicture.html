Big Picture: Integumentary System & Healing

Comprehensive synthesis connecting all Week 5 topics: skin as first defense, wound healing as universal model, infection prevention, immune function, nutrition, and psychological impact.

Integumentary System as First Defense

Skin Structure and Function

Anatomy and Immune Role

  • Epidermis: Outermost layer; provides waterproof barrier; contains melanocytes (pigment/UV protection)
  • Dermis: Contains collagen, blood vessels, sensory nerves, hair follicles, sweat glands; provides mechanical strength
  • Subcutaneous Tissue: Fat layer; insulation; shock absorption
  • Barrier Function: pH, resident flora, lysozyme, and antimicrobial peptides prevent pathogen colonization
  • HIV Manifestations on Skin: Kaposi Sarcoma (HHV-8, CD4 < 50), oral candidiasis (CD4 < 200), HSV/VZV recurrence, seborrheic dermatitis; skin changes indicate immune status
  • Skin as Immune Indicator: CD4 < 200 = high OI risk; visible skin conditions guide opportunistic infection screening

Wound Healing as Universal Model

Three Phases Apply Across All Tissue Injury

Hemostasis/Inflammatory → Proliferative → Maturation

  • Acute Surgical Wounds: Follow standard healing; sutures removed week 1-2; full strength ~6 months
  • Chronic Ulcers (Pressure/Diabetic): Prolonged inflammatory phase; impaired perfusion/immune response delays proliferation; may never reach full strength
  • Burn Wounds: Massive tissue destruction; prolonged inflammatory phase; infection risk high; require active debridement and grafting
  • Skin Cancers & Kaposi Sarcoma: Tissue destruction extends deeper; healing impaired at low CD4; ART restoration of immunity critical for recovery
  • Moist Wound Environment: Universal principle across all wound types; speeds healing 2x; promotes autolytic debridement

Infection Prevention as Unifying Theme

Multi-Level Defense Strategy

Preventing Complications Across HIV, Wounds, and Burns

  • Topical Antimicrobials: Silvadine/silver nitrate for burns; antifungals (nystatin) for oral candidiasis; antivirals for HSV
  • Sterile Technique: Central line care, wound dressing changes, urinary catheter care prevent iatrogenic infection
  • Surgical Debridement: Remove necrotic tissue (burns, ulcers, Kaposi) to prevent bacterial colonization and toxin production
  • Systemic Antibiotics: Culture-guided therapy for purulent wounds, cellulitis, burn sepsis, TB coinfection
  • Immune Restoration: ART for HIV restores CD4 count → resolves OIs (candidiasis, MAC prophylaxis can be stopped at CD4 > 50-100); prevents future infections
  • Prophylaxis by CD4 Count: PCP (CD4 < 200), MAC (CD4 < 50), toxo (CD4 < 100), CMV monitoring (CD4 < 50)

Nutritional Support as Foundation

Protein, Vitamins, Minerals Critical for All Healing

Nutritional Requirements and Deficiency Consequences

  • Protein: Essential for collagen synthesis; deficiency = impaired wound strength, slow epithelialization; needs 1.5-2.5 g/kg in acute wounds/burns
  • Vitamin C: Cofactor for collagen cross-linking; deficiency = fragile collagen, poor scar formation; supplement 250-500 mg daily in wounds
  • Vitamin A: Essential for epithelialization; deficiency = poor skin healing, recurrent infections; supplement if deficient
  • Zinc: Cofactor for numerous enzymes; deficiency = impaired immune function, slow healing; supplementation if low
  • Calories: Hypermetabolism in burns/sepsis requires 2-3x normal intake; malnutrition prolongs healing weeks-to-months
  • HIV Wasting Syndrome: Severe malnutrition at low CD4; ART partially reverses; nutritional supplementation + appetite stimulants necessary

Psychological Impact & Holistic Care

Mental Health as Critical Component of Recovery

Psychosocial Considerations Across Week 5 Topics

  • Visible Skin Conditions (Acne, Eczema, Rosacea): Body image disruption, social anxiety, isolation; impact on self-esteem and quality of life
  • Skin Cancers & Kaposi Sarcoma: Cancer diagnosis triggers fear of mortality, metastasis; Kaposi visible indication of advanced AIDS, stigma, grief
  • Burn Survivors: PTSD from trauma, depression, body image disruption from scars/contractures, social reintegration challenges; long rehabilitation affects psychosocial wellbeing
  • HIV Diagnosis: Stigma, fear of disclosure, relationship concerns, grief about health/future; psychological screening at baseline and ongoing
  • Nursing Role: Mental health screening (PHQ-9 for depression, GAD-7 for anxiety); referral to psychology/psychiatry; support groups; education to reduce stigma; family involvement; celebration of recovery milestones
