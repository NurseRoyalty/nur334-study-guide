Exam 4 Jeopardy

Built entirely from this site's Week 6 and Week 7 content: GI Diagnostics & Bariatric Surgery, GERD, Hiatal Hernia & PUD, GI Bleed, IBD & Obstruction, Colorectal Cancer & Ostomies, and Diverticular Disease (Week 6), plus Cancer, Cholelithiasis & Cholecystitis, Pancreatitis, Cirrhosis & Hepatitis, and Hepatobiliary Labs & Diagnostics (Week 7). Every question is NCLEX-NGN/clinical-judgment style and comes with an answer and a rationale. Nothing comes from outside this material.

This is a 2-player game. Each round randomly draws 3 Week 6 categories and 3 Week 7 categories (30 questions in all), and questions get harder from 100 to 500 points in every category. Click a value, read the question, then press Reveal Answer; the answer and rationale stay hidden until you do. Then add or subtract points for Player 1 or Player 2. New Game draws new categories and new questions each time, and Reset Game replays the current board from scratch.
