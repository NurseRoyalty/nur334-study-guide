Must Know Quick Reference

A ★ marks something the instructor said outright would be on an exam.

Week 1 Content

First Sub-Topic

  • ★ A thing she said you will be tested on — stated in about twenty words, with the specific number or threshold included.
  • A second point: high-yield, no star, because she never said it would be tested.

Second Sub-Topic

  • Group related facts into one bullet rather than splitting them — a bullet that carries a definition, its threshold, and its consequence is one reference item, not three.
  • Keep the wording tight. Drop the mechanism asides, the percentages you won't be asked for, and "she said…" on any bullet that already has a star.

Not Tested

Explicitly Excluded

  • Things the instructor said you do not need to memorize — exact doses, specific percentages, brand names, and so on.
