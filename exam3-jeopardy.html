Exam 3 Jeopardy

Built entirely from this site's Week 4 and Week 5 content: Urinary Tract Infections, Pyelonephritis, Nephrolithiasis, Acute Kidney Injury, and Chronic Kidney Disease (Week 4), plus HIV Diagnosis & Labs, HIV ART & Prevention, Skin Conditions, Wounds & Complications, and Burns (Week 5). Every question is NCLEX-NGN/clinical-judgment style and comes with an answer and a rationale. Nothing comes from outside this material.

This is a 2-player game. Each round randomly draws 3 Week 4 categories and 3 Week 5 categories (30 questions in all), and questions get harder from 100 to 500 points in every category. Click a value, read the question, then press Reveal Answer; the answer and rationale stay hidden until you do. Then add or subtract points for Player 1 or Player 2. New Game draws new categories and new questions each time, and Reset Game replays the current board from scratch.
